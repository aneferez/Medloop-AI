MedLoop AIMedicine reminders and family care coordination

Privacy policy

Effective 4 July 2026

Data MedLoop handles

MedLoop stores account details, medicine routines, dose logs, family contacts, appointments, prescription notes, profile photos, prescription images, and settings on this device. Device reminders are scheduled locally.

How information is used

Information is used to unlock the local account, schedule medicine reminders, show adherence history, and prepare user-reviewed SMS or WhatsApp messages. MedLoop does not sell personal information.

Cloud services

This local-only build does not use Firebase, Firestore, Cloud Functions, hosted AI, analytics, crash reporting, App Check, Remote Config, Hosting, or cloud storage. Local-only mode cannot send email verification messages, password-reset links, or sign in with Google.

Family messages

Missed-dose and refill messages are prepared locally and opened in the device's SMS application or WhatsApp for review. MedLoop does not silently send messages. WhatsApp is a separate service with its own privacy terms.

Retention, choices, and deletion

Local records remain until edited or deleted. Users can disable reminders, SMS drafts, and WhatsApp messages. Account deletion removes the local MedLoop account and records on the current device. See the account deletion instructions.

Security

MedLoop keeps this build's data on the device and disables Android cloud backup. No system can guarantee absolute security, so users should enter only the information needed for medicine coordination.

Medical disclaimer

MedLoop is a reminder and organization tool. It does not provide diagnosis, medical advice, emergency monitoring, or treatment recommendations. Always follow the original prescription and confirm unclear instructions with a qualified clinician or pharmacist.

For privacy questions, use the developer contact provided on the MedLoop AI Google Play listing.
