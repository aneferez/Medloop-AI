MedLoop AIAccount and data deletion

Delete your account

Updated 3 July 2026

Delete directly in the app

  1. Sign in to the MedLoop account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Scroll to Delete account.
  4. Confirm with your local account password, then select Delete my account.

What is deleted

The local MedLoop account and records on the current device are permanently removed, including medicine routines, dose logs, family members, appointments, alerts, prescription notes, profile photo, prescription images, and settings.

Device-local information

Information on another device cannot be removed remotely. Uninstall MedLoop or clear its application storage on each device where you used the app.

If you cannot access the app

Local-only accounts cannot be deleted remotely. If you cannot access the app, clear MedLoop's app storage from Android settings or uninstall the app.

Read the privacy policy

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
