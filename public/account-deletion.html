MedLoop AIAccount and data deletion

Delete your account

Beta notice · Updated 30 August 2026

Cloud-enabled account deletion is being finalized in the beta backend. Do not assume that removing local data removes synced records.

Delete directly in the app

  1. Sign in to the MedLoop account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Scroll to Delete account.
  4. Confirm with your local account password, then select Delete my account.

What is deleted

When available, the completed deletion flow must remove the account, medicine routines, dose logs, family members, appointments, alerts, prescription notes, profile photo, prescription images, linked devices, push tokens, and cloud copies. Until a confirmed cloud deletion receipt is shown, local deletion only removes data from the current device.

Device-local information

Information on another device may remain in its local app storage. Uninstall MedLoop or clear its application storage on each device where you used the app, and contact the developer for synced-record deletion requests during the beta.

If you cannot access the app

If you cannot access the app, contact the developer using the privacy contact on the MedLoop AI Google Play listing and include the account email. Do not send prescription images or other medical details in the request.

Read the privacy policy

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
